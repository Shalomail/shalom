These terms apply to anyone who creates a Shalom Mail account. They're short on purpose: what we offer, what we expect from you and what happens if something goes wrong.

1. The service

Shalom Mail is an encrypted email service with a @shalomail.com address. Accounts are free and come with 1 GB of storage.

We may change features and limits over time. When a change affects something you use, we'll tell you in the app first.

With the CLI, your account can create email mailboxes for AI agents: 2 at no cost, and more with the Agents 5 (up to 5 mailboxes, US$ 4.99 a month), Agents 12 (up to 12 mailboxes, US$ 9.99 a month) and Unlimited (unlimited mailboxes, up to 250 new a day, US$ 49.90 a month) plans. The subscription is monthly, renews automatically and can be cancelled whenever you want in the billing portal; it stays active until the end of the paid period. You can withdraw within 7 days of subscribing and get your money back. Payment is processed by Stripe. You are responsible for what your agents send, as if you had sent it.

2. Your account

You must be at least 13 years old, or the minimum age required in your country, to create an account. If you're under 18, use it with the guidance of a parent or guardian.

Addresses that differ only by dots, hyphens or underscores count as the same address, so no one can register a lookalike of someone else's.

You're responsible for your password, your PIN and your recovery codes, and for everything done with your account.

3. Your password is the key

Your stored emails only open with a key that is locked by your password. We don't have a copy of that key.

So if you forget your password, we can't recover your stored emails. That isn't a flaw in the service: it's what guarantees that only you can read them. Keep your password safe and, if you turn on two-step verification, your recovery codes too.

4. What isn't allowed

Don't use Shalom Mail to:

  • send spam or bulk messages nobody asked for;
  • scam, phish or impersonate another person or company;
  • spread viruses or any malicious software;
  • harass, threaten or stalk anyone;
  • share any child sexual abuse material;
  • break the law or violate other people's rights;
  • get around limits, break into accounts or attack the service.

There are sending limits, such as the number of recipients per day, to protect the people who use the service and the reputation of our addresses.

5. Suspension

We may limit or suspend accounts that break these terms. Abuse like spam and scams shows up in sending volume and reports, without us having to read the content, which stays encrypted.

When possible, we'll warn you first. If you think it was a mistake, write to contato@shalomail.com.

6. What you write is yours

We claim no rights over what you write or receive. Since it's encrypted, we can't even see it.

7. No guarantee of perfect operation

We do our best to keep the service running and secure, but it's provided as is, with no guarantee that it will work without interruption or errors. Keep a copy of anything that matters to you.

To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses, or for lost emails caused by a forgotten password or by access made with your sign-in details.

8. Closing your account

You can delete your account whenever you want, at the bottom of Settings, or ask us at contato@shalomail.com. When an account is deleted, its stored emails, agent mailboxes and account data are removed and the plan is cancelled. The address stays reserved and is never used by anyone again.
