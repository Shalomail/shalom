Frequently asked questions

Short, direct answers about Shalom Mail, the encrypted email service at shalomail.com.

The account

What is Shalom Mail?

Shalom Mail is an encrypted email service that works in the web browser, at shalomail.com. The account is free, with 1 GB and a @shalomail.com address. Stored email is locked with a key that only exists on your device, mail between Shalom accounts is end to end, and AI agents can have mailboxes of their own.

Is Shalom Mail free?

Yes. The email account is free, with 1 GB of storage and a @shalomail.com address, and there are no ads. The only paid plans are for people who need more than 2 mailboxes for AI agents.

Do I need a phone number or an ID to sign up?

No. You choose an address and a password, and Shalom asks for no phone number and no ID. Before your first mailbox opens, it asks for a recovery email at another provider, which is how you get back into the account if you forget your password. The server keeps it locked and uses it only to send you a link.

Can I email people on Gmail or Outlook?

Yes. A Shalom address sends to and receives from any provider, such as Gmail, Outlook, iCloud or Yahoo, and people there get your messages as normal email. Between Shalom accounts, mail is end to end.

Is there an app to install?

No. Shalom Mail works in the web browser, on computers and phones, so there is nothing to install. Mailboxes for AI agents are used with the shalom command line tool or the Node SDK.

Which languages is Shalom Mail available in?

15 languages: Portuguese, English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Hebrew, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

Who runs Shalom Mail?

Can I delete my account?

Yes. In Settings, Delete account, on the website or in the app: stored email, agent mailboxes and account data are deleted, and any paid plan is cancelled. Infrastructure backups may take a few days to expire.

Privacy and encryption

Can Shalom read my email?

Not your stored email. Every message is sealed with a key that only exists on your device, and the server keeps it without being able to open it. Email from other providers can be read by the server at the moment it arrives, before it is sealed, and email you send to other providers goes out as regular email.

How the encryption works

Is Shalom Mail end-to-end encrypted?

Between Shalom accounts, yes. The message is sealed on the sender's device and opens only on the recipient's, and even the sender is hidden from the server. With other providers it cannot be, because regular email travels unencrypted, so Shalom seals those messages the moment they arrive.

How the encryption works

What happens if I forget my password?

Your stored email cannot be recovered, because only your password opens your key and Shalom has no copy of it. With your recovery email you can set a new password and keep your address, but the mailbox starts empty.

Which encryption does Shalom Mail use?

X25519 keys with HKDF-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM for every stored message, Argon2id for the password on your device, Ed25519 certificates for the sealed sender and RSA blind signatures for anonymous delivery between Shalom accounts.

Is Shalom Mail's code open?

It is open for audit: anyone can read the code on GitHub, build it and check that it is the same code that runs at shalomail.com. It is not open source, because the license does not allow reusing the code in another service. Security problems can be reported to security@shalomail.com.

See the code on GitHub

Does it use PGP?

No. Shalom has its own format, built on X25519, HKDF-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM, and does not use PGP keys. Email to other providers goes out as regular email.

Does it show ads or track me?

No. There are no ads and no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and your email is not used to train anything. Images that report when you opened an email are blocked, and fake senders show up in amber.

Does Shalom hand data to the authorities?

Only under a valid court order. What the server has is listed in the privacy policy. The content of stored email stays encrypted, and Shalom does not have the key.

Email for AI agents

How do I give an AI agent its own email address?

Install the shalom CLI with npm install -g shalomail, connect it to your account with shalom login, confirming in the browser, and run shalom agents create with a name. The agent gets name@shalomail.com and can read, reply to and send real email, with JSON output.

Email for AI agents

How much do mailboxes for AI agents cost?

2 come free with any account. Agents 5 costs US$ 4.99 a month for up to 5, Agents 12 costs US$ 9.99 a month for up to 12, and Unlimited costs US$ 49.90 a month, with no mailbox limit and up to 250 new mailboxes a day. Plans are monthly, billed by Stripe, and can be cancelled at any time.

Can Shalom read my agents' email?

Not their stored email. Each agent mailbox has its own private key, created on the machine where you run the CLI or the SDK, and the server keeps its mail sealed. Your account key can create and delete agent mailboxes but cannot open them.

Is there an API or an SDK?

Yes. The shalomail package on npm is both the CLI and a Node SDK, with the Agent and Account classes, and there is an HTTP API. Encryption always happens on your side.

Can an agent react to new email on its own?

Yes. shalom watch --exec runs your program once for each new message, with the message as JSON on standard input, and agent.watch() does the same in the SDK. There are no webhooks yet.

Can agents email people outside Shalom?

Yes, as regular email. To outside addresses, each agent mailbox sends to up to 20 recipients a day in its first week and up to 100 a day after that. Between Shalom addresses, mail is end to end and the sender is verified.
