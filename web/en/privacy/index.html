Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Shalom Mail is operated by X TECH A.I FUND HOLDING E PARTICIPACOES LTDA, based in Governador Celso Ramos, SC, Brazil.

Shalom Mail was built to know as little about you as possible. Here, plainly, is what the service stores, what it can't see and what you can ask for.

1. Who looks after your data

Shalom Mail is the controller of the data processed in this service under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018) and, for people in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

To talk about privacy: privacidade@shalomail.com.

2. What is encrypted and no one but you can read

  • Your stored emails: body, subject, sender, attachments and which conversation each belongs to. All of it is locked with your key.
  • The key that opens your emails sits in an envelope locked by your password, opened only on your device. The server keeps the envelope closed, with a second lock of its own, and has no way to open it.
  • Your password never reaches the server. Your device derives a proof from it, and we only store a code made from that proof.

3. What the server needs to know to work

  • Account: your address, the name you chose and, if you add one, your profile photo. The photo is public to anyone who knows your address, as with other email services.
  • Recovery email: the non-Shalom address you gave us, stored locked and opened only to send you the confirmation link and the new password link. Screens show only a masked version. It is removed when you change it or delete your account.
  • Security: your PIN and recovery codes stored in a way that can't be read back, your two-step verification secret locked, and the inactivity time you chose for automatic sign-out.
  • Each message: which account it's for, when it arrived, how much space it takes, which folder it's in and whether it has been read, pinned or starred.
  • Connected devices: when each session started, when it was last used and the type of browser, so you can see them and sign them out.
  • iPhone notifications: if you turn on notifications in the iOS app, the identifier Apple gives the device, tied to that iPhone's session, so we can send the new message notification.
  • IP address: used on the spot to stop abuse, like password guessing and mass sign-ups, and kept only in counters that expire within 1 hour.
  • API keys and agent mailboxes: each key stored in a way that cannot be read back, when it was last used, and which agent mailboxes belong to your account.
  • Paid plan: your customer and subscription identifiers at Stripe, the plan and when it renews. Card details stay with Stripe and never pass through our servers.

4. Email to and from outside Shalom

Email from another service arrives unencrypted, like all email on the internet. The moment it arrives, the server checks it for spam or scams and locks it with your key. The unencrypted version is not stored.

Email you send to another service goes out as regular email and passes unencrypted through the sending service to its destination.

End-to-end encryption, with no unencrypted step at all, applies between Shalom accounts. In that case not even the sender's name is visible to the server.

5. Cookies and browser storage

We only use what's necessary: the session cookie, which keeps you signed in; the known-device cookie, which skips the anti-bot check where you've signed in before; and preferences stored in your browser, such as theme, language and open accounts. Your key can also be stored in the browser, locked, if you choose to remember the device.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

6. Who we share data with

The infrastructure runs on Cloudflare: servers, database, storage, receiving and sending email, and the anti-bot check (Turnstile). Cloudflare processes data on our behalf; stored emails reach it already encrypted.

If you subscribe to a paid plan, billing is done by Stripe, which receives your Shalom address and payment details to process the subscription, under its own privacy policy.

If you turn on notifications in the iOS app, each one goes through Apple's notification service (APNs). Apple receives the device identifier, the time and the text "New message", with no sender, subject or anything from the message.

Cloudflare's servers are in many countries, so your data may be processed outside Brazil, with the safeguards required by Brazilian data protection law.

We don't sell data, we don't show ads and we don't use your emails to train anything.

We only hand data to authorities under a valid court order. What we have is what's listed in section 3: the content of stored emails stays encrypted, and we don't have the key.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and messages: as long as the account exists. Trash and Spam empty themselves after 30 days.
  • Sessions: up to 30 days without use, or less if you choose a shorter inactivity sign-out.
  • iPhone notification identifier: for as long as that iPhone's session exists. It is removed when you sign out, disconnect the device or turn notifications off.
  • Counters with IP addresses: up to 1 hour.
  • Plan: while the account exists. Stripe keeps payment records for as long as the law requires.
  • When an account is deleted, its data is removed. Only the address is kept, so no one can register it again and receive what was meant for you. Infrastructure backups may take a few days to expire.

8. Your rights

You can ask for confirmation that we process your data, access, correction, portability, deletion and information about who we share it with, and you can withdraw any consent you gave.

You can delete the whole account, with its stored email, agent mailboxes and data, at any time in Settings, Delete account, on the website or in the app.

You can change your name, photo and security settings in the app itself. For anything else, write to privacidade@shalomail.com. You can also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or to the authority in your country.

9. Security

Besides encryption, we use a second server-side lock on the key envelope, sign-in attempt limits, an anti-bot check, optional PIN and two-step verification, and cookies that only work on this site and over a secure connection.

No system is foolproof. If an incident could affect you, we'll tell you and notify the ANPD, as the law requires.

10. Children

Shalom Mail is not for children under 13. If you know of an account belonging to a child, write to privacidade@shalomail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Shalom Mail (shalomail.com) is an encrypted email service that works in the browser. Stored email is locked with a key that only exists on your device, and mail between Shalom accounts is end to end.
